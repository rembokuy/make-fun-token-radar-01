How the implied value is worked out

implied FDV = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity × chain

  • Peer multiple — for each category, the median market-cap ÷ TVL of the snapshot’s own live-token protocols in that category. A category with fewer than 3 live peers uses the global median instead. No protocol name is ever special-cased; every figure comes from the snapshot.
  • Growth — 1 + clamped 7-day TVL change ÷ 100 × 0.5, with the change clamped to −60% … +120%.
  • Maturity — 0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.4. A protocol tracked longer counts for more; unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Chain — 1 + min(chains − 1, 3) × 0.05, up to 1.15: a protocol live on more chains counts for more.

Global peer multiple: — (median mcap ÷ TVL across all live-token protocols). Rows are ranked by implied value, highest first; ties break by slug.
